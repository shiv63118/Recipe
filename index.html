Italian

Creamy Pasta

A rich and creamy pasta perfect for dinner.

🛒 Ingredients

Pasta, cream, garlic, butter, cheese, salt and pepper.

👨‍🍳 How to Make

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until tender.
  2. Heat butter and sauté garlic.
  3. Add cream and cheese, then stir well.
  4. Mix in pasta and cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Serve hot.

Breakfast

Fluffy Pancakes

Soft, fluffy pancakes perfect for breakfast!

🛒 Ingredients

Flour, milk, eggs, sugar, baking powder and butter.

👨‍🍳 How to Make

  1. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.
  2. Add milk and eggs, then whisk smoothly.
  3. Heat a pan and add butter.
  4. Pour batter and cook both sides.
  5. Serve with syrup.

Healthy

Fresh Garden Salad

A healthy, colorful and refreshing dish.

🛒 Ingredients

Lettuce, tomato, cucumber, carrot, olive oil and lemon juice.

👨‍🍳 How to Make

  1. Wash and chop all vegetables.
  2. Place them in a bowl.
  3. Add olive oil and lemon juice.
  4. Toss everything well.
  5. Serve fresh.

Indian

Vegetable Biryani

Aromatic rice cooked with vegetables and spices.

🛒 Ingredients

Rice, mixed vegetables, spices, onion and yogurt.

💡 Tip: Use basmati rice for the best results.

👨‍🍳 How to Make

  1. Cook rice until it is 70% done.
  2. Prepare the spiced vegetable mixture.
  3. Layer the rice and vegetables.
  4. Cook on low heat (dum) for 15 minutes.
  5. Serve hot.

Quick Snack

Grilled Sandwich

A crispy, cheesy snack ready in minutes.

🛒 Ingredients

Bread slices, butter, cheese, vegetables and chutney.

👨‍🍳 How to Make

  1. Spread butter and chutney on bread.
  2. Add vegetables and cheese.
  3. Cover with another bread slice.
  4. Grill until golden brown.
  5. Cut and serve hot.
